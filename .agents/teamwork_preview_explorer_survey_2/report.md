## 2. Feature Representation & Column Architecture

### 2.1 Metadata & Identifier Columns
`deap_features_replication.parquet` contains 4 non-feature columns:
- `subject` (`str`): Participant identifier (`'s01'` to `'s32'`).
- `trial` (`int64`): Original trial index within the DEAP experiment (0 to 39).
- `segment` (`int64`): Segment index within the trial (0 to 15, corresponding to the sixteen 3.75-second non-overlapping windows of the 60-second trial).
- `target` (`int64`): Binary ground truth label (`1` for Stress, `0` for Calm).

### 2.2 EEG Feature Columns (640 total)
There are exactly 32 EEG channels $\times$ 20 extracted features = **640 feature columns**, named with the format `CH{channel}_{feature}`.

#### The 20 Features Extracted Per Channel:
1. **Time Domain (7 features)**:
   - `line_length`: Total waveform length ($\sum |\Delta x|$)
   - `kurtosis`: Fourth standardized statistical moment
   - `peak_to_peak`: Peak-to-peak amplitude difference ($\max(x) - \min(x)$)
   - `skewness`: Third standardized statistical moment
   - `hjorth_activity`: Signal variance $\sigma^2_x$
   - `hjorth_mobility`: $\sqrt{\text{var}(x') / \text{var}(x)}$
   - `hjorth_complexity`: Ratio of mobility of first derivative to mobility of signal
2. **Frequency Domain (5 features)**:
   - Relative band powers calculated via Welch's Power Spectral Density (PSD, normalized by total power):
     - `rp_theta`: $\theta$ band ($4 - 8\text{ Hz}$)
     - `rp_alpha`: $\alpha$ band ($8 - 12\text{ Hz}$)
     - `rp_sigma`: $\sigma$ band ($12 - 15\text{ Hz}$)
     - `rp_low_beta`: Low $\beta$ band ($15 - 20\text{ Hz}$)
     - `rp_high_beta`: High $\beta$ band ($20 - 30\text{ Hz}$)
3. **Time-Frequency & Non-Linear Domain (8 features)**:
   - `wavelet_energy_d1`: Energy of Daubechies 4 (db4) detail coefficient D1 ($32 - 64\text{ Hz}$)
   - `wavelet_energy_d2`: Energy of db4 detail coefficient D2 ($16 - 32\text{ Hz}$)
   - `wavelet_energy_d3`: Energy of db4 detail coefficient D3 ($8 - 16\text{ Hz}$)
   - `wavelet_energy_d4`: Energy of db4 detail coefficient D4 ($4 - 8\text{ Hz}$)
   - `wavelet_energy_d5`: Energy of db4 detail coefficient D5 ($2 - 4\text{ Hz}$)
   - `wavelet_energy_d6`: Energy of db4 detail coefficient D6 ($1 - 2\text{ Hz}$)
   - `spectral_entropy`: Normalized Shannon entropy of the power spectrum
   - `katz_fd`: Katz's Fractal Dimension (signal complexity measure)

### 4.2 Cross-Validation Feasibility Analysis

#### Trial-Level vs Segment-Level Cross Validation:
1. **Trial-Level Stratified 10-Fold CV is Impossible**:
   - For a 10-fold stratified split to execute, every class must have at least 10 samples so that each fold contains at least 1 sample of each class ($N_{\text{minority}} \ge 10$).
   - At the trial level:
     - The maximum minority trial count in the entire dataset is only 7 trials (in `s10`).
     - Subject `s26` has only 2 trials in total (1 Stress, 1 Calm).
     - Subjects `s18` and `s27` have only 1 Stress trial.
     - Hence, trial-level 10-fold CV cannot run on ANY subject.
2. **Segment-Level Stratified 10-Fold CV is 100% Mathematically Feasible**:
   - Because each trial is segmented into 16 windows of 3.75 seconds, 1 trial = 16 segment samples.
   - The lowest minority class segment count in the 25 subjects is **16 segments** (occurring in `s18`, `s26`, and `s27`).
   - For a 10-fold split with 16 samples: 6 folds receive 2 minority samples, and 4 folds receive 1 minority sample. Every fold has at least 1 minority class sample.
   - All other 22 subjects have $\ge 32$ minority class segments.
   - Therefore, `StratifiedKFold(n_splits=10, shuffle=True, random_state=42)` executes cleanly without exceptions for all 25 subjects.

#### Methodological Implication (Intra-Trial Leakage vs Paper Alignment):
- Dividing trials into segments and applying random StratifiedKFold means segments from the same 60s trial can appear in both training and testing folds.
- This is the exact segmentation methodology established by Hag et al. (2023) and implemented in `replicate_paper.py`.
- To prevent numerical errors or runtime warnings in `precision_score` or `recall_score` on small folds, `zero_division=0` is required.

## 5. Readiness for Advanced Model Training & Feature Engineering Roadmap

### 5.1 Parquet Features Readiness Verdict
The features in `deap_features_replication.parquet` are **structurally complete and ready for model consumption**, requiring NO raw EEG re-extraction. However, raw consumption without proper pipeline transformers will cause performance degradation.

### 5.2 Mandatory Pipeline Requirements for R2 & R3:

1. **Strict Fold-Isolated Feature Scaling**:
   - Features exhibit extreme scale divergence ($\Delta > 10^7$).
   - Models such as SVM, KNN, RLDA, LogisticRegression, and Neural Networks require standardization.
   - `StandardScaler()` or `RobustScaler()` must be `fit` strictly on `X_train` and applied via `transform` to `X_test` within each CV fold to eliminate data leakage.

2. **Per-Subject Class Imbalance Handling**:
   - While the global dataset is well balanced (54.6% vs 45.4%), individual subjects are severely skewed:
     - `s27`: 88.9% Calm (16 Stress vs 128 Calm)
     - `s14`: 84.6% Stress (176 Stress vs 32 Calm)
     - `s24`: 77.8% Stress (112 Stress vs 32 Calm)
     - `s13`: 76.9% Stress (160 Stress vs 48 Calm)
     - `s18`: 75.0% Calm (16 Stress vs 48 Calm)
   - Baseline RLDA evaluation reveals that `s27` suffers a precision of **28.33%** and recall of **30.00%**, and `s18` suffers a precision of **48.33%**, severely depressing the macro-averaged metrics.
   - **Remedy**: Incorporate `class_weight='balanced'` in SVM/RF/LogisticRegression, `scale_pos_weight = N_neg / N_pos` in XGBoost/LightGBM, or per-fold oversampling (SMOTE/RandomOverSampler) inside the training folds.

3. **Dimensionality Regularization ($p \gg n$)**:
   - The sample size per subject ranges from $N=32$ (`s26`) to $N=272$ (`s22`).
   - Using all 640 features creates severe over-parameterization ($p \gg N$), leading to overfitting.
   - Solutions:
     - Evaluate the **8 Universally Optimal Channels** (160 features).
     - Apply per-fold feature selection (e.g., `SelectKBest(f_classif, k=60)` or Mutual Information) fitted strictly inside training folds.
     - Leverage L1/L2 regularized classifiers or tree ensembles with feature bagging (`colsample_bytree <= 0.7`).

4. **Model Architecture Recommendations to Exceed 85% Accuracy**:
   - Advanced Tree Ensembles: **XGBoost** with `tree_method='hist'`, `subsample=0.8`, `colsample_bytree=0.7`, `learning_rate=0.03-0.08`, `max_depth=4-6`.
   - **LightGBM / CatBoost** with early stopping or bagging.
   - **Ensemble / Stacking Classifier**: Blending tuned SVM (RBF kernel, $C \in [1, 10]$), Regularized LDA (`shrinkage='auto'`), and XGBoost with soft voting (`voting='soft'`).
   - **Subject-Averaged Benchmarking**: Compute fold-level accuracy, precision, and recall for each of the 25 subjects, average across the 10 folds to produce per-subject metrics, and report the unweighted macro mean across all 25 subjects.
